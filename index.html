Privacy Policy

Part 1

1. Information Collection and Use

Timber Mode Calculator is designed to work entirely offline.

  • We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, location data, or calculation history.
  • All calculations are processed locally on your device and are never sent to any external server.
  • The app does not require internet connectivity for its core functionality.

2. Device Permissions

The app is built to be clean and non-intrusive. It does not request or access any sensitive device permissions, including but not limited to:

  • Location (GPS)
  • Camera or Photos
  • Microphone
  • Contacts
  • Push Notifications (unless explicitly opted in for service status alerts, with no data collected)

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

We do not integrate any third-party advertising networks, tracking SDKs, or analytics engines that compile your personal usage patterns. Your activity within the app remains entirely private.

4. Children's Privacy

Timber Mode Calculator is a general-purpose measurement utility suitable for a general audience, including teenagers, and is not specifically directed at young children. As the app collects no personal data from any user, no additional data is collected from minors either.

5. Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the app or applicable law. Any changes will be posted on this page along with a revised "Last updated" date. It is your responsibility to review this page periodically; continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
